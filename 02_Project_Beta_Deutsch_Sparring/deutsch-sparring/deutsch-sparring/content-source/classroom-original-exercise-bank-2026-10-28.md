# EC-003 · Original classroom-inspired exercise bank (A1–A2)

**Status:** Draft exercise bank on the working branch; **not yet active in wort.**
**Date:** 2026-10-28
**Source:** Emilio's 45 photographed workbook pages and handwritten notes, catalogued in [EC-002](classroom-photo-inventory-2026-10-22.md).
**Copyright approach:** Extract **grammar concepts, pedagogical goals and generic task types** from the photos, then create entirely **new prompts, people, contexts, answers and dialogues**. No book page, original illustration, exercise wording, substantial answer sequence or book-specific story has been reproduced.
**Interpretation of levels:** A1 forms are taught with predictable everyday exchanges; A2 uses freer explanations, cases and reactions to new information. A structure can appear at both levels with different demands.

These tables are teacher-facing editorial source material. On integration, route the question and model answer to the existing lesson or `lib/curriculum.js` topic; do not blindly expose all of the following open responses in a fixed-answer demo. Use the app's supported grammar exercise types as appropriate, and accept other grammatically and contextually correct answers.

## Pack 01 · A1 · Imperativ: du, ihr, Sie

**Photo themes/index:** 01–03, 10 (see EC-002 inventory). **Curriculum destination:** `g-a1-imperativ`.

|---|---|---|---|

**Interactive transfer:** Your neighbour is sleeping. Ask someone politely to turn down the music, then thank them.

## Implementation/quality notes

- **Not copied:** Photo indices refer only to the *source learning theme*. Prompt wording, fictional scenarios, item ordering, answers and transfer questions are newly written.
- **Not a one-to-one transcription:** Several photographs revisit the same grammar concept; their educational content is consolidated into one coherent practice pack, rather than recreated photo by photo.
- **Not yet part of the running app:** This content belongs to `content-source/`. The curriculum and lesson data under `lib/curriculum.js` and `content/` remain untouched until the planned review and integration.
- **Multiple acceptable answers:** Model solutions are illustrative; free-response tasks require semantic evaluation, not exact string matching.
- **One key issue in the material:** `wo?` vs `wohin?` only predicts the dative/accusative contrast for **two-way prepositions**. `zu`, for example, always governs the dative.
- **Source-text limits:** The copyrighted pages remain in Emilio's upload, not in the repository. Use only independently authored pedagogical exercises in the application.
- **Coverage:** 16 grouped exercise packs and 128 original draft questions, designed from all major photographed themes. The exercise bank is a substantial first conversion, not proof that every example on every page has been recreated.
