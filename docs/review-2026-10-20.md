# wort. – review 20 Oct 2026

This review covers the whole app: how it is built and hosted (architecture), security, the screens (UX), and how well it teaches German. I read the code and ran the app with `MOCK=1`. I also ran small test scripts. I changed nothing. Every finding below was checked a second time, and severities were corrected where the first check overstated them.

**What is good**
- The app is well organised. AI calls have safety nets: timeouts, retries, a backup model and JSON answers.
- Word scores are calculated in one file (`lib/scores.js`), on the server.
- Passwords are hashed with a slow method (scrypt), so a stolen password list is hard to crack. The login cookie is signed and the browser cannot read it with scripts.
- Demo mode (`MOCK=1`) works without an AI key. All texts are in `lib/ui-text.js`. All 6 check scripts pass.
- Nothing is critical and nothing is high. No data is being lost and nothing has leaked today.

**The 5 most important problems**
1. **The class code can be guessed without limit, and the usage limits do not hold on Vercel.** Wrong codes sent to the chat and coach are never counted. Limits are kept only in the server's memory. One person can use up the free AI allowance for the whole class, or fill the database. (S1–S5)
2. **Preview links use the live database.** Code on an unfinished branch reads and writes real students' data before you have approved it. (A1)
3. **"Coach not available" has real causes in the code.** When the AI returns an empty or cut-off answer, the app gives up instead of trying the backup model. The temperature setting goes against Google's advice. The coach prompt allows 6 changes, but redoing a plan needs 8. (A10–A13)
4. **Grammar practice is too easy for B1, and the Swiss Perfekt "ich bin gestanden" is treated as wrong.** A missing file holds the tutor at A1–A2 grammar, and every noun task tells you the case. (T1, T8, T9)
5. **Goals measure the wrong thing.** A milestone ticks when one overall percentage passes a number. Single words show up as "grammar gaps", and the summary contradicts itself. This is why the goals redesign below exists. (T13, U6, U7)

**What to do first:** R1 (limits that hold), then R2 (a separate preview database), then R3 (saves that cannot be half-done). After that, R4 fixes most "coach not available" errors.

## How to use this document

It works exactly like `docs/revamp-tasks.md`:
- **One task = one new Claude Code session = one branch = one pull request.** Do the tasks in order. Each starts after the previous pull request is merged.
- Pick the **model** shown for the task (`/model`), then paste the task's grey box. Nothing else is needed.
  - **Haiku:** small, clear changes in 1–3 files. Cheapest.
  - **Sonnet:** normal features.
  - **Opus:** only where a mistake is expensive or hard to see (security, rewiring core data).
- If a Haiku or Sonnet session gets stuck twice on the same error, re-run the task with the next model up.
- Every prompt points to **"Common rules" in `docs/revamp-tasks.md`**: read CLAUDE.md, ask questions all at once, wait for your "approved", build on a branch, `npm run build` and `MOCK=1` must work, update the README, and give you the preview link.
- File paths below are inside the app folder `02_Project_Beta_Deutsch_Sparring/deutsch-sparring/deutsch-sparring`, unless they start with `docs/`.
- Finding IDs (A1, S1, U1, T1 …) link the findings to the tasks.

## Goals, sub-plans and milestones – redesign

*This merges the teaching, UX and architecture designs into one spec. I re-checked the facts the three designs disagreed on:*
- *The curriculum has **34 topics**: 12 for A1, 11 for A2, 11 for B1 (`lib/curriculum.js`). R26 adds more later.*
- *Verbs **do** store their Perfekt, in `words.json` → `enriched.perfekt`, for example `"ist abgebogen"`. Verbs with Perfekt "ist": A1 10, A2 12, B1 13.*
- *Today's limits are `MAX_MILESTONES = 12` (`lib/goals.js:13`) and `maxBytes: 20_000` (`app/api/goals/route.js:11`).*
- *`WINDOW_DAYS = 30` (`lib/home-stats.js:18`) and `SHARE_NEW = 0.3` (`lib/scores.js:38`).*

### 1. Summary for Emilio

**What is wrong today.** A milestone like "Exam readiness 25 %" is ticked when one overall percentage for the whole goal passes 25. It does not depend on what the milestone says. Three more problems:
- Progress only looks at the last 30 days. So a milestone can turn green and later go back to "missed".
- A "Pass Goethe B1" goal ignores your 761 A1 and A2 words.
- Grammar answers are not linked to a topic. So "Genitiv" cannot be measured on its own.

**What changes:**
- **Goal** is what you want, with an end date. Example: "Pass the Goethe B1 exam by 24 Apr 2027".
- **Part** (called *sub-plan* in the code) is one building block. Examples: "A2 vocabulary", "B1 grammar", "Exam practice".
- **Milestone** is one piece of real content with a date. Examples: "Food & drink (A1): 43 of 45 words learnt", "Wechselpräpositionen: 16 of last 20 right". The app measures it. Once reached, it **stays reached**.
- **Session** is one practice round. It is started for one milestone, with topic, level and settings filled in.

**Who builds the plan.** The app builds it from fixed rules: your word lists, the grammar topics in teaching order, your minutes per day and your end date. The coach (the AI) only explains the plan, picks today's session from the app's milestones and suggests changes. It never makes up titles, numbers or dates.

**What you will see.** Percentages disappear from the screen. You see counts of real things: "38 of 45 words", "3 of 11 topics", "2 of 3 mock exams".

**Cost.** No new package and no new database. The existing database gets one new column on goals and three small fields on answers. You approve this in decision D7.

### 2. Learning design

#### 2.1 Part types

| Part (`kind`) | Contains | Measured as | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vocab` | the words of one level (optionally one section or list, one source), in chunks | "212 of 455 words learnt" | every word goal, exam goals |
| `grammar` | the curriculum topics of one level, in prerequisite order | "7 of 12 topics solid" | grammar goals, exam goals |
| `exam` | mock exams and Schreiben/Sprechen tasks | "1 of 3 mock exams done" (you tick them) | exam goals only (on by default, D4) |
| `check` | a short placement check per level, in the first 1–2 weeks | "A1 words checked: 455 of 455 seen" | exam goals, optional (D1) |
| `manual` | your own or old-style steps, ticked by hand | ticked / not ticked | migrated goals, "Build my own" |

There is no *review* part. Words that are due show inside the vocab part ("12 words due") and decide today's session (§4.6).

**How each kind of goal maps:**
- "Improve my A1 vocabulary" = `vocab A1`
- "Practise A2 grammar" = `grammar A2`
- "Learn Home & living" = `vocab`, one section, every level up to the goal's level
- "Wechselpräpositionen" = `grammar A2` with only that topic
- "Course words" = `vocab`, source `kurs`
- "Pass Goethe B1" = `check` (optional) + `vocab A1, A2, B1` + `grammar A1, A2, B1` + `exam`
- "Pass Goethe A2" = the same, up to A2

**Order.** Inside a level, vocabulary and grammar run **in parallel**. Levels go in order, lowest first: A1 → A2 → B1.

#### 2.2 Grammar: order, prerequisites, role

One milestone per curriculum topic. Topics follow the Schritte plus Neu order, and a topic is never scheduled before the topics it needs. "→" means "needs first".
- **A1:** artikel-plural, personalpronomen, praesens, wortstellung → praesens, trennbare-verben, modalverben, akkusativ → artikel-plural, possessivartikel, zeit-ort, dativ-praepositionen → akkusativ, imperativ, perfekt.
- **A2:** perfekt-partizip → a1-perfekt, praeteritum-sein-haben-modal, verben-dativ, dativ-akkusativ-objekte, wechselpraepositionen, reflexive-verben, weil, dass-wenn → weil, adjektivendungen → wechselpraepositionen, komparativ-superlativ, konjunktiv2-hoeflich.
- **B1:** praeteritum, konjunktiv2, verben-praepositionen, relativsaetze, nebensaetze, zu-infinitiv, zweiteilige-konnektoren, plusquamperfekt → praeteritum, passiv, genitiv, n-deklination → genitiv.

The prerequisite list goes into `lib/curriculum.js` as `needs: []`. *Implementation check: these lines name 31 topics (A1 12, A2 11, B1 8). Before step 3, check every `needs` id against the ids in `lib/curriculum.js`, and put the three B1 topics not named here into the order.*

Each topic gets a **role**:
- **Productive** (you must write and say it yourself in the exam): every A1 and A2 topic, plus B1 konjunktiv2, verben-praepositionen, relativsaetze, nebensaetze, zu-infinitiv and praeteritum. Threshold **80 %**.
- **Receptive** (you must understand it, mostly in Lesen): passiv, plusquamperfekt, genitiv, n-deklination, zweiteilige-konnektoren. Threshold **70 %**.
- **adjektivendungen** is productive, but uses **70 %**. It is hard even for B1 learners.

#### 2.3 Vocabulary: order and chunks

**Section order inside a level** (daily-life needs first, the same order as the Goethe themes):
1. Person & identity
2. Family & relationships
3. Time & calendar
4. Food & drink
5. Home & living
6. Clothes & shopping
7. Transport & travel
8. Health & body
9. Communication & services
10. Work & education
11. Leisure, culture & nature

**Chunk rule.** One chunk is one milestone:
1. Start with one section at one level.
2. If it has more than 40 words, split it into its lists.
3. Merge a list with fewer than 8 words into the next list of the same section ("Food & drink: Bread, Dairy, Basic foods (A1) – 9 words").
4. Split a list that still has more than 40 words into parts of about 25, by word id ("Verbs of action (B1) – part 2 of 4").
5. **Each word belongs to only one milestone per part**: the first one in this order. The milestones then add up to the part total, so no word is counted twice.

**Interleaving.** The big lists are not placed as a block at the end: Verbs (×3), Function words & expressions and Common adjectives. They are cut into parts, and each week has one of these parts next to a noun chunk. Verbs and function words carry the grammar.

**Membership is worked out live** from `words.json` by one fixed function. Words added by the weekly import join their chunk, shown as "+4 new words". A reached chunk stays reached.

**Source.** For an exam goal, Goethe words are the core target. Course-only words of the same level are an extra chunk per section, marked "optional" (D2). Other goals include every source, unless you choose `kurs` only.

#### 2.4 How progress is measured

All of these rules live in one file, `lib/plan-progress.js`.

**Words**
- **Learnt** = V ≥ 8, the same `LEARNT_FROM` the tile uses. The goal average (V−1)/9 is no longer shown anywhere, so "learnt" has only one meaning.
- **Word chunk reached** = at least 90 % of its words learnt (D3). The screen shows "43 of 45 learnt", then "all 45 ✓" at 100 %. Why 90 %: a word at V = 8 or more drops below 8 after one wrong answer (`scores.js:35`), so "all" would keep bouncing.

**Grammar topics** are counted per curriculum topic id, after the data fix in §4.4. These answers count:
- answers on Mittel or Schwer;
- an answer with a hint counts as half right;
- a skipped answer counts as wrong. Today a skip is left out (`home-stats.js` `aggregateAnswers`), which rewards skipping.

The topic states are:
- **Introduced** = 10 or more counted answers, 60 % or more right. This is a progress state only: "Introduced · 12 answers, 67 %".
- **Solid** reaches the milestone. It needs all three of these:
  - at least 20 counted answers;
  - spread over at least 2 different days;
  - the **last 20** answers at 80 % or more (productive) or 70 % or more (receptive).
  - The screen shows "16 of last 20 right · needs 16", or "12 answers so far · needs 20".

**Measured on the last N answers, never on a 30-day window.** Old mastery does not disappear, and new mistakes still show quickly. The 30-day window stays only for the "recent" tiles.

**Milestones never go back.** When the rule is met, the app stores `reachedAt`.
- Words: if more than 20 % of a reached chunk is due or below 8 → the state is **Needs a refresh**, shown as "Reached 3 Nov · 5 words due".
- Grammar: if the last 10 answers on a reached topic fall below 70 % → also **Needs a refresh**.

The tick stays in both cases. The coach may suggest one refresh session.

**Already solid.** A milestone that is already met when the goal is made is marked "Already solid ✓", with `reachedAt` set to the creation date and no due date.

#### 2.6 Pace

**You choose minutes per day:** 15 / 20 / 30 / 45 (default 20). The plan assumes 6 practice days a week.

**What things cost:**
- A new word to learnt: about 6 answers.
- Keeping learnt words: about 0.1 answers per learnt word per week.
- A grammar topic: about 50 answers to solid.
- Time per answer: about 0.5 min for a flashcard, about 1.5 min for a sentence task. After 14 days the app uses your real median per task type, measured from `answers.created_at`.

**Weekly budget:** vocabulary 50 %, grammar 35 %, exam skills 15 %. The exam share only applies once the `exam` part has started.

|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | 15 | 1 every 2 weeks | 1 a week |
| 20 | 20 | 1 every 1.5 weeks | 1–2 a week |
| 30 | 30 | 1 a week | 2 a week |
| 45 | 40 | 1 a week (never two new topics reaching solid in the same week) | 2 a week |

### 4. Technical design

#### 4.1 Data model

**One new column, `goals.plan jsonb`.** The browser's `wort_goals` uses the same shape.
- Saving becomes **one UPSERT** (a single statement that inserts or updates). This ends the delete-then-insert gap in A2.
- Limits:
  - up to 8 parts per goal;
  - up to 40 milestones per part, which replaces `MAX_MILESTONES = 12`;
  - `/api/goals` `maxBytes` raised from 20 000 to 60 000;
  - `MAX_GOALS = 12` stays.

```js
goal = {
  id, version: 2, title,
  template: 'exam'|'vocab'|'grammar'|'wordtopic'|'topic'|'kurs'|'custom',
  level: 'A1'|'A2'|'B1', startDate, endDate, isExam: bool,
  options: { sources: 'goethe'|'kurs'|'both', section?, list?, topicIds?: [] },
  pace:    { minutesPerDay: 20 },             // the generator works out the rest
  subPlans: [{
    id, kind: 'vocab'|'grammar'|'exam'|'check'|'manual', level|null, order, include: true,
    milestones: [{
      id, date|null, order,
      criterion,                               // see below; title is derived, never stored from the model
      status: 'open'|'skipped',
      reachedAt: null|'YYYY-MM-DD', alreadySolid: bool,
      origin: 'template'|'coach'|'student',
      result?: { lesen?, hoeren?, schreiben?, sprechen? },   // mock points 0–100
      note?: ''
    }]
  }]
}
```

**Criteria** (`lib/plan.js` `cleanCriterion`):
- `{kind:'words', level, section, lists:[..]|null, part:{i,n}|null, sources, optional:bool}`. Membership comes from `chunkWords(subPlan, words)` (§2.3).
- `{kind:'grammar', topicId, target:'solid'|'introduced'}`. The threshold comes from the topic's role in `curriculum.js` and is never stored per milestone.
- `{kind:'exam', part:'mock'|'lesen'|'hoeren'|'schreiben'|'sprechen', n, lessonId?}`
- `{kind:'manual', text}`
- Phase 2: `{kind:'placement', level, sources}` and `{kind:'verbs', levels:[..], aux:'sein'|'haben'|null, count}`

**Answers table (D7)**, three new columns that may be empty:
- `topic_id text`
- `hinted boolean`
- `difficulty text`

One new index: `answers (user_id, topic_id, created_at DESC)`. These columns are added with the numbered migrations from R3 (`ALTER TABLE … ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`).

**The browser log** entry `{i,r,o,s,w,t}` gets `g` (topic id), `h` (hinted) and `d` (difficulty). A new store, `wort_rulelog`, keeps `{topicId: [[ok, hinted, t], … last 40]}` with no 30-day cut.

**Sessions:** `sessions.settings.milestoneId` (inside the jsonb that already exists).

#### 4.2 Generating the plan (`lib/plan-templates.js`, server only)

`buildPlan(draft, { scores, grammarStats, today })` uses fixed rules only (no AI call), so MOCK=1 works.
1. Make the parts from the template (§2.1).
2. Make the milestones: word chunks (§2.3), one per grammar topic in `needs` order (§2.2), and the exam items.
3. Mark milestones that are already met: `alreadySolid` + `reachedAt = today`.
4. Schedule two parallel tracks, vocab (A1 → A2 → B1) and grammar (A1 → A2 → B1), by workload and pace (§2.6). The exam items go at about 30 %, 60 % and 90 % of the time, with weekly tasks from the halfway point. Exam goals keep the 2-week buffer.
5. Return `tight: { needsUntil, fixes: [...] }` when the plan does not fit.

`replan(goal, stats, today, levers)`:
- keeps reached milestones, coach and student milestones, and left-out milestones;
- re-dates the open template milestones from today to the end date;
- the levers are `minutesPerDay`, `endDate` (not for an exam goal), `focus:[subPlanId]` and `cut:['kursWords'|'receptiveIntroduced']`;
- returns a list of concrete changes.

`sessionForMilestone(m)` turns a milestone into session settings:
- **Words** → `{kind:'vocab', topicId: wordTopics() id, level, sources, questions:20, pool: chunk word ids}`. Grammar point: articles and plural on first contact, then Akkusativ/Dativ for nouns, Perfekt for verbs.
- **Grammar** → `{kind:'grammar', topicId, level}`, 20 questions. Difficulty:
  - Leicht while the topic is below 50 % of the "introduced" mark;
  - Mittel by default;
  - Schwer once the last 20 on Mittel are at 80 % or more.
- **Exam** → the lesson id plus the task in the Sparring message.

#### 4.3 Measuring progress (`lib/plan-progress.js`, pure functions)

**Input:** `{ goals, words, scores, grammarStats, today }`.

**Output:**
- per milestone: `{ have, need, unit, state, refresh }`;
- per part: `{ reached, total, nextId, behindDays }`;
- per goal: `{ nextMilestoneId, status: 'ontrack'|'behind'|'ahead'|'done'|'ended', progressShare }`.

`progressShare` is the share of reached milestones, weighted by work. It is used inside the app only. `goalSnapshot` and the summary keep working until they move to the milestone wording.

**`grammarStats`:**
- **With an account:** `progress().grammarStats(userId)` returns the last 40 counted answers per `topic_id`, using `row_number() OVER (PARTITION BY topic_id ORDER BY created_at DESC)`. Skips count as wrong, a hinted answer as 0.5, and Leicht answers are left out.
- **Without an account:** from `wort_rulelog`, sent with the existing `/api/stats` POST (400 kB limit).

**Latching (a reached milestone is saved and stays reached):**
- With an account, `homeData` stores newly reached ids through `goalsStore().markReached(userId, goalId, ids)`. It sets `reachedAt` only where it is still empty, so running it twice does no harm.
- Without an account, `/api/stats` returns `newlyReached` and `home-client.js` saves them locally.

**Behind** (worked out once a day) means any of these:
- more than 1 week behind the pace, using a straight line between milestone dates;
- an open milestone whose date has passed;
- 7 or more days with no practice.

**Ahead** means more than 2 weeks ahead.

#### 4.4 Data fixes the measuring needs

1. **Clean the rule name at the source.** In `normalizeReply` (`lib/reply.js`), match `feedback.rule` against the session topic's `ruleNames`, then against all curriculum `ruleNames` and `RULES`. The match ignores upper/lower case and extra spaces. If nothing matches → `rule = null`. Words such as "der Schrank" then no longer count as grammar (T13). Today the word is written in at `reply.js` and in `word-tasks.js` (the `rule = w.de` lines).
2. **Give each answer a topic** when it is saved:
   - In a curriculum grammar session → the session's `topicId`.
   - Otherwise → the lowest-level topic whose `ruleNames` contain the cleaned rule. This settles rule names shared by two topics, such as "Perfekt mit sein" in `g-a1-perfekt` and `g-a2-perfekt-partizip`.
   - Old answers: filled in once by the migration, joining to `sessions.settings->>'topicId'`, with the same fallback.
3. **Store** `hinted` (already read at `progress.js:97`) and `difficulty` in the answers INSERT in `progress.js`.
4. **Fix the coach facts:** `CURRICULUM_WORDS` → real counts (§2.6). Exam goals include every lower level (today `home-stats.js` counts only `level: goal.level`).

#### 4.5 Coach (`lib/coach-prompt.js`, `lib/coach.js`, `lib/coach-mock.js`)

**What the coach is told:** goal → parts, with live counts and status → the **next 3 open milestones** per part, with ids and what is still missing ("7 words not learnt: der Schrank, …" / "Genitiv: 14 of 20 answers, 71 %"). Also the weekly budget, the real word counts, and the exam items marked "outside the app". The percentage lines go. Earlier coach turns that carried a plan are sent with a short summary of that plan (A15).

**Answer format:**
- `suggestedSession: { milestoneId }`. The server fills in the settings with `sessionForMilestone`. If the coach gives no milestone or an unknown one, the server uses the next milestone instead.
- `planChanges: { goalId, replan: {minutesPerDay?, endDate?, focus?, cut?} | null, changes: [{ action: 'move'|'skip'|'restore'|'addPart', subPlanId, milestoneId?, date?, partKind?, level? }] }`. There are no `title` or `target` fields.
  - The criterion fields are flat, because Gemini's schema handles mixed shapes badly.
  - `addPart` may only use the part types in §2.1.
- Checks (`cleanPlanV2`):
  - the ids exist and the dates pass `dateOk`;
  - an exam goal's end date never changes;
  - reached milestones are never moved, left out or removed;
  - thresholds never change.
- `replan` is turned into concrete changes on the server. *Apply* checks everything again against the current goal.

**Fixes the coach suggests when you are behind, in this order, at most once a week unless you ask:**
1. More time for the part that is behind; no dates change.
2. Move its open milestones into the buffer.
3. Cut scope, naming exactly what goes: course-only words, then receptive topics set to "introduced", then rare lists.
4. Move the end date. Only you do this, and never for an exam goal.

**Old saved coach messages** with plan changes in the old format are shown as `replaced`.

**`coach-mock.js`:**
- greeting → the next milestone of the goal that is most behind;
- "less time" → `replan({minutesPerDay})`;
- "weakest" → gaps, now without words.

#### 4.6 Today's session (one function, used by Next up, the coach and the mock)

1. More than 40 words due → a review session of due words across all lists. This needs a new mode in the word picker; until step 8 it falls back to point 2.
2. Otherwise → the next open milestone of the part that is most behind.
3. Otherwise → the next milestone in plan order, vocabulary and grammar on alternate days.

## Word score rules (31 Oct 2026)
Decided by the owner for R21 (T10, T11, T22); later sessions follow them without asking again. Code: `lib/scores.js`.
1. Any wrong answer makes that part due tomorrow, whatever the new score (a skip is not a wrong answer).
2. A 10: each further right check doubles the gap, 21 → 42 → 84 days, cap 180 (168 → 180). The gap is stored in the score (`iv`; database columns `v_iv a_iv p_iv g_iv`, migration step 6). Right after a hint keeps the gap.
3. Overdue 10s are always in the due group of a session (not limited by CHECKS_MAX).
4. In "Vocabulary + grammar" sessions a missing Sentence score counts as weak (not for phrases).
5. Meaning (v) can rise at most once per calendar day per word (`up` / `v_up`); wrong answers still lower it.
All rules apply to every part (Meaning, Article, Plural, Sentence) except 4 and 5, which are about the Sentence and Meaning scores only.
